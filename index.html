🗿 Archaeologists of Mastodon 🔬

One selected panel of a woolly mammoth, from the Rouffignac cave. Courtesy of the Bradshaw Foundation.

You want to get in touch with archaeologists from around the world on Mastodon? This page lists accounts from archaeologists and archaeological institutions and offers an easy method to follow multiple accounts at once. You are provided with a .csv-file which you can upload into Mastodon to follow all accounts included. Here you can find a short instruction on how to do this.

This list is also available for browsing via: Find Sciences.Social. Visit academics-on-mastodon for additional academic Fediverse resources.

➕ Add Yourself

Sign up via the Google Form (preferred) or DM @NikaShilobod@fediscience.org, @andreatitolo@archaeo.social, or @RonaldVisser@akademienl.social. Additions are usually processed within 24 hours. Full names are only included if shown publicly in your profile.
